Audit rules and deferred decisions

  • Do not treat every candidate as installed. Verify Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge compatibility and check overlap before adding.
  • Big Globe is a separate major-overhaul experiment, not an ordinary worldgen addition.
  • Keep Integrated Stronghold; exclude YUNG's Better Mineshafts and Moog's Mineshafts Reimagined.
  • Luki's Crazy Chambers remains parked after earlier worldgen/initialization problems.
  • Keep Sophisticated Backpacks and Storage; avoid reintroducing unwanted GUI replacements.
  • Worldgen should stabilize before major progression and questbook balancing. Questbook planning can happen in parallel.
