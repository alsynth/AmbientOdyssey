## Dedicated server pregeneration and Questlog player tips — **approved plan, not implemented**
- User's server plan is **pregenerate/preload chunks before opening the real 5–7-player world** so normal travel in the initially supported region encounters existing chunks rather than paying the full FreeTerraForged/Streams generation cost in real time.
- Important vocabulary distinction: **chunk pregeneration** permanently creates chunk data on disk; **force loading** keeps chunks actively loaded and costs ongoing RAM/CPU; **view distance / Distant Horizons / Voxy LOD** is separate from the existence of generated Minecraft chunks.
- The admin should choose the server launch seed, complete mods/terrain config, and agreed primary exploration boundary **first**, then backup and pregenerate **Overworld** with a **compatible, verified 1.21.1 NeoForge server pregenerator** (evaluate Chunky or equivalent; not pinned/installed yet). Evaluate pregen in other heavily explored dimensions after ensuring their worldgen is final.
- **Never pregenerate before worldgen/content mods and biome configuration are stable.** Changing terrain and structures *after* pregeneration yields old/new chunk seams and missing new content in pre-created terrain.
- Pregeneration can consume many hours, CPU, RAM, storage and I/O; stage the radius, stop/start safely, track TPS/heap and drive health. Back up the world before batch runs. Verify that the specific tool actually generates fully populated chunks for FTF+Streams+multidimensional world. Server-side pregen reduces future heavy **new** chunk generation, but will **not** eliminate every lag source (entities, exploration beyond boundary, client shaders/LOD, map loads, block updates).
