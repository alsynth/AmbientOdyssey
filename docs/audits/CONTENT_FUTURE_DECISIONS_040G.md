# Ambient Odyssey — 0.4g deferred content decision register

**Date:** 10 October 2026. **Status:** For user review ONLY upon request. No auto-pins for these entries, no unsolicited bulk candidate dumps. Historical registry of 166 research candidates is preserved; names can already be installed via inherited baseline even if they did not get selected in this specific wave.

## Policy
Choose additions based on differentiated content, believable difficulty and loot, adequate NeoForge 1.21.1 support, nonredundant gameplay, dependencies and worldgen safety. User permitted independent selection; no need to force every candidate. **Never merge experimental branch into main or generate permanent world before runtime validation.**

## Choices accepted for 0.4g
- **Dimensional Dungeons 210** — Repeatable instanced-feeling puzzle/combat dungeon dimension
- **Ancient Remnants: Monoliths 1.5.2** — Rare monoliths, blessings, codex investigation and exploration rewards
- **TarotCards: Remastered 2.3.5** — 22 collectible Major Arcana cards found in structure loot
- **Aquaculture Delight 1.2.0** — Aquaculture 2 fish recipe integration with Farmer's Delight kitchens
- **End Remastered Additions 1.2.0** — Additional End Remastered eyes tied to fishing, trial chambers, sculk, Aether and BOMD

## Remain postponed by judgment (representative)
- **Critters & Companions:** native version exists, but AO already has Naturalist, Alex's Mobs, Cosy Critters, aquatic mobs; direct species/spawncap review needed before adding more roly-polies, snails and other ambient creatures.
- **The Birth of Steve, EpicQuestDungeoning, dimensions and more bosses:** baseline already has many distinct dimensions and combat systems; Dimensional Dungeons is one reproducible server-oriented dungeon system, compare it first instead of stacking all dungeon frameworks.
- **Pufferfish/NeoOrigins and many alternative skill trees:** choose one player progression identity, no two competing trees. Content-first selection can be revisited after current new content runtime acceptance.
- **Tarot balancing:** install content now; reduce deck/effect overstack during the scheduled balance phase. Default upstream 22 cards can be exceptionally powerful with Apotheosis.
- **Connected texture engines, DH/Voxy, shader changes, biome/river overhauls:** do not randomly change render pipeline or worldgen in this content wave.
- **End Remastered rare Potato Eye:** mod offers optional discovery paths; do not impose collecting every eye or force near-impossible potato farming.
