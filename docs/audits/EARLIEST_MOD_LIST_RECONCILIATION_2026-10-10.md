| Quests and narrative | FTB Quests base; research for Easy NPC and discovery UI | As requested, quest writing including archaeology and custom Questlog comes **last** |

## Highest-value unfinished expansion choices

**1. Discovery/settlement payoff:** structure/biome discovery tracking, collectible journals, rewarding archaeological dig sites, NPC/world interactions (actual quest writing deferred). **2. Player growth:** choose one skills/origins tree with meaningful magic/Apotheosis bridges. **3. Oceans and dimensions:** diversify with existing providers before risking a second terrain generator. **4. Animal habitats:** fix eligibility, species duplication and spawncap impacts, not pointless pests. **5. Boss and loot ecology:** tune rather than flood. **6. Config-first optimization** and multiplayer backups, then **quests**. Keep MineColonies/Galosphere excluded and Big Globe/MCA Reborn/extra bridges parked.
