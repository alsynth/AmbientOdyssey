## What the JAR scan found (328 JARs)
14,175 loot tables (1,149 chest, 865 entity), 18,417 structure templates, 322 loot-modifier files in 41 JARs. 188 parse errors, of which 182 were one scanner bug since fixed (Biomes We've Gone structures); a rerun would add them.

1. **Spawners:** 1,979 classic spawners in 825 templates (WDA 130, IDAS 73, Graveyard 68, Moog's Nether 67, Philip's Ruins 51, Moog's Soaring 50). They spawn 65 entity types, mostly vanilla (skeleton, zombie, wither skeleton, cave spider); only 160 (8%) carry equipment NBT, mostly WDA (95), Seven Seas (26), IDAS (21).
2. **Trial spawners and vaults are a separate world:** 178 templates use trial spawners (Dungeons & Taverns 58, Adventure Dungeons 65, WDA 17, Iron's 10, Luki's Woodland Mansions 9, Moog's Nether 8 / Soaring 7). Dungeon Difficulty cannot change their spawner parameters (it has no trial-spawner code), but mobs they spawn still get location-based attribute scaling when the structure zone matches. 157 have the config inline in structure NBT and only 6 trial-spawner data JSONs exist (Moog's Nether 4, Soaring 2), so only those are datapack-editable. 94 templates have vaults (Dungeons & Taverns 33, Archaion 17).
3. **Apotheosis loot injection reaches only part of the dungeons.** Apotheosis matches the loot-table *path* with a full-match regex (`chests.*`) and **the first matching entry wins** (source-verified). Of 9,846 chest references in structure templates, 61.6% match. Near-zero: Moog's Soaring 0%, Temples Reimagined 0%, Structory 0%, Formations Overworld 0%, Bumblezone 0%, Incendium 1%, Philip's Ruins 1%, Moog's Voyager 4%, Luki's Grand Capitals 7%. Partial: Dungeons & Taverns 54%, Trek 55%, Adventure Dungeons 64%. WDA, IDAS, Graveyard, Iron's, Cataclysm, Mine Cells and Moog's Nether are ~100%. The separate affix-conversion modifier (35% per eligible weapon/armor item, every table except blocks) covers the rest.
4. **Many chests use vanilla tables**, so a vanilla override would also change vanilla (Tier 0-2) structures: Dungeons & Taverns 360 references (35 vanilla tables), Towns & Towers 351, CTOV 226, Trek 220, Create: Structures Arise 110, Explorify 102, Adventure Dungeons 72, Additional Structures 46, Cataclysm 42. A location- or structure-conditioned loot modifier (not tested) would be the alternative.
5. **Per-chest tables already exist in several mods:** WDA 143 distinct tables over 338 chest templates, IDAS 160, Dungeons & Taverns 155, Integrated Villages 133, Trek 132, CTOV 93, Iron's 37. Cataclysm has only 21 and Create: Structures Arise 13, so per-chest levels there need template or conditional-modifier work.
6. **Boss drops are data tables for most boss mods:** Cataclysm (ignis, scylla, harbinger, leviathan, ender guardian, golems), Mowzie's (frostmaw, wroughtnaut, umvuthi, sculptor), Ice and Fire (9 dragon tables), F.D. Bosses (5), Block Factory's (8), Remnant (hollow sovereign, umbrakar, kotsukage), Aquamirae, Twilight Forest, Eternal Starlight, Mine Cells (concierge, conjunctivius), Iron's. **BOMD** has entity tables only for Lich and Void Blossom; Obsidilith and Gauntlet rewards are arena *chest* tables (`chests/obsidilith`, `chests/gauntlet`). Remnant ships duplicate namespaces (`remnant_bosses:` and `remnants:`); which one is live is unverified. Ancient Remnants has no combat loot tables.
7. **Loot injection is wide:** 322 modifier files in 41 JARs (about 29 JARs have modifier types whose names look like loot adders; name heuristic). Largest: Artifacts 37, Enigmatic Legacy+ 41, Traveloptics 20, Quark 18, Iron's Spells 18 (+9 Iron's Jewelry), Farmer's Delight 31 loot adders, Forbidden Arcanus 11, Cataclysm Spellbooks 11, Sophisticated Backpacks 9, Deeper and Darker 7, Deep Aether 5. This is on top of Apotheosis, Loot Integrations, Dungeon Difficulty and Lootr. Their order and interaction were not tested.

## Runtime results: can boss stats be edited? (AOT runs 1 to 4, 13 Oct 2026, after owner review)
Method: each boss entity gets +300% `max_health` and `attack_damage` attribute modifiers (the mechanism Dungeon Difficulty, Gateways and Apotheosis use), 30 seconds before and after, 1024-HP player, self-test passed in every run, vanilla husk control scaled exactly 4x (HP 20 to 80, hit about 3 to 12). Run 4 (`aot:start_needed`) re-measured 42 bosses with the corrected `execute summon`. `results.csv` is built by `tools/aot_build_results.py`: baseline and HP from the latest run, **damage = best verdict across runs**, because one 30-second sample cannot capture a boss with mixed attacks (verdicts for the same boss changed between runs for ender_guardian, scylla, fire_dragon, ice_dragon, lightning_dragon). The owner removed non-bosses and marked interaction-locked bosses manual (`AOT_ROSTER_DECISIONS.csv`); 62 bosses remain.
- **HP: the attribute route works on 60 of 61 bosses with a usable reading.** 27 reached exactly 4x; **33 hit the vanilla 1024 max-health limit** (slider, valkyrie_queen, atlatitan, hullbreaker, luxtructosaurus, tremorzilla, captain_cornelia, last_of_deepslate, wilden_boss, kraken, lich, obsidilith, void_blossom, ancient_remnant, ender_guardian, ignis, maledictus, netherite_monstrosity, scylla, the_harbinger, the_leviathan, ancient_ancient_remnant, lich, fire_dragon, ice_dragon, lightning_dragon, dead_king, fire_boss, hollow_sovereign, kotsukage, ossukage, umbrakar, hydra). A boss with base HP of 256 or more cannot be quadrupled; one near 1000 gains 2 to 8%. Raising the cap needs an AttributeFix-style mod; none is installed. Exception: **FD Bosses' Malkuth** reads 1024 at spawn and 80 after the modifier, so it rewrites its own max health and the attribute route is unreliable for it.
- **Damage (53 bosses with hits in some run): 29 scale (21 about 4x, 8 clearly scaling), 11 only partly, 13 not at all.** Not affected: slider, infernal_dragon, kraken, the_leviathan, eots_controller, lunar_monstrosity, chesed, malkuth, lich, ossukage, umbrakar, hydra, ur_ghast. Partial: tremorzilla, sandworm, lich, ignis, netherite_monstrosity, permafrost, geburah, dead_king, umvuthi, kotsukage, alpha_yeti. Their hits use fixed numbers, projectiles, minions or separate parts (Hydra's heads). Some bosses recorded no hits in the latest run but scaled in an earlier one (Archaion, Slider, Leviathan: they attack only after an aggro/phase trigger).
- **Manual (9):** sun_spirit, valkyrie_queen, void_worm, yeti, gauntlet, ancient_remnant, the_harbinger, starlight_golem, the_gatekeeper. They need a special interaction, an arena or a trigger that a command cannot reproduce.
- **Baselines now validated:** with spawn setup running, all 14 bosses whose HP is known from config or source match it exactly (Sandworm 200, Underworld Knight 280, dragons 500 at 125 days of growth). The old `/summon <id> ~ ~ ~ {NBT}` skipped spawn setup and gave wrong baselines for Scylla (390, really 1024), Malkuth (20), Naga (120, really 200), Captain Cornelia (20, really 300), Sandworm, Underworld Knight and the dragons.
- **Caveats:** FD Bosses' fights run on hit counters, so their attribute results say little about the real fight; minion and projectile hits count as the boss's; this proves the generic attribute route only, not the mods' own config keys, phases or abilities; difficulty normal.

- **F.D. Bosses has no HP value:** Chesed/Malkuth use hit counters (10 each); per-attack damage is configurable. **Aether** exposes no boss stat config.
- **Dungeon Difficulty 3.9.2** (source): attributes (add / multiply-base), classic spawner parameters, XP, looted item stats, player effects only. No mob equipment, abilities, trial spawners or drop contents. Config path `config/dungeon_difficulty/difficulty_v2.json` (README stale). Applies loot scaling through a global loot modifier.
- **Apotheosis 8.9.0** (source `7dd7cec`): 31 invaders, 4 elites, data-driven per-rarity stats. Overworld zombie invader: Uncommon +20-60 HP, Rare +40-90, **Epic +80-150 HP / +30-65% damage, Mythic +110-180 HP / +70-105%**. Spawn chance by tier: Haven 0, Frontier 0.018, Ascent 0.02, Summit 0.025, Pinnacle 0.03 (Overworld). Rarity weights: Epic from Frontier (10) to 650 at Pinnacle; Mythic 0 until Summit (50), 250 at Pinnacle. **Tier unlocks:** a full five-slot set of the right rarity plus a milestone (Ascent: kill an invader; Summit: kill the Wither; Pinnacle: kill the Ender Dragon). Affix conversion also applies to boss and mob drops.
- **Iron's Spells:** per-chest loot tables (Pyromancer tower has 7) and four boss-drop variants including `_per_player`. **Gateways to Eternity:** data-driven waves with attribute/gear modifiers and loot-table rewards on any mob.
- **AO today** overrides one loot table and one loot modifier through Paxi (proven route); structure placement is already overridden for WDA, Farmers, dragon caves and F.D. Bosses.

## Decision implications
- **Slower Apotheosis progression and stronger Epic/Mythic invaders:** both are data changes (rarity weights, injection chances, tier advancements, invader stats). Because the injection matches table paths, it will not touch the 0% mods above unless you add entries for them.
- **Different chests, different reward levels:** easy in WDA, IDAS, Dungeons & Taverns, Trek, Integrated Villages, Iron's (distinct tables); hard where chests share vanilla tables (Create: Structures Arise, Explorify, Cataclysm partly).
- **Tier 0-2 untouched:** never override `minecraft:` tables for modded-structure tuning. **Verified in the live instance:** Dungeon Difficulty runs AO's pilot, with only 6 Overworld structure zones (Dungeon Crawl dungeon, Adventure Dungeons trial rooms, WDA Illager Fort / Keep Kayra / Plague Asylum, Iron's Catacombs), +12% HP and +7% damage per level, loot scaling off, spawner scaling off, no dimension default; so vanilla and other structures are unscaled.
- **Dungeon Difficulty as the central scaler:** spawner-parameter scaling only helps classic-spawner dungeons (WDA, IDAS, Graveyard, Moog's, Philip's Ruins); trial-spawner dungeons (Dungeons & Taverns, Adventure Dungeons, Luki's Mansions) can still get mob attribute scaling but not spawner changes. Its scaling is attribute-only, so scripted bosses can ignore it.
- **Boundaries:** ordinary Apotheosis data, the Apotheotic Additions prototype and the armor-trim addon are separate JARs; a loot change should name which it touches. Mine Cells' live config has no mob or boss stat keys (11 keys in `common.json5`; the `value_calculators` are player-weapon formulas), so its bosses cannot be tuned through config.
